Our project workflow

1. Field Capture

Collect customer information, job photos, preparation needs, and painting scope during the site visit.

2. Estimates and Customer Approval

Prepare an estimate with selectable options. Customers review the scope, choose their work, and sign online.

3. Crew Work Orders

Give the assigned crew clear instructions based on the approved scope of work.
